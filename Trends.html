ChatGPT & Conversational AI

ChatGPT is an advanced AI chatbot developed by OpenAI, designed to engage in human-like conversations and assist with a wide range of tasks. Launched on November 30, 2022, it has become one of the fastest-growing consumer software applications in history, reaching over 100 million users within two months of its release.

ChatGPT continues to trend due to its rapid growth, innovative features, and widespread integration across various sectors. As of early 2025, it has become a central tool in digital marketing, e-commerce, and even personal relationships.

Enhanced Learning

Tutors powered by ChatGPT aid student learning across subjects.



Project Turntable

Project Turntable leverages machine learning to transform flat vector illustrations into 3D-like visuals. By simply clicking a button and adjusting a slider, users can rotate their artwork, and the AI intelligently fills in any missing parts, such as hidden limbs or back details, maintaining the integrity of the original design.

In a quick five-minute stage presentation at the MAX conference, Adobe researcher Zhiqin Chen starts with a 2D vectorized Illustrator scene of a warrior fighting a dragon. The warrior is staring directly out of the screen, though, and turning him to face the dragon on his left would usually require "redraw[ing] the entire shape, which is going to take a lot of time," as Chen points out.

360° Visual Generation

It can take a single image and generate a full turntable-style rotation—giving the illusion of a 3D model without actually needing a 3D scan.

AI-Powered Consistency

The model understands object geometry and lighting, generating consistent, realistic views that align smoothly as the object "spins".

Faster Product Visualization

Great for ecommerce, marketing, and design mockups—it drastically cuts the time and cost needed to visualize products in multiple angles.
